## What I do `/work-with-me`
Meta description: Three ways to work with Kalid Nur: find the gap, build the fix, and teach your team to run it.

- Kicker: WHAT I DO
- H1: Three ways to work together
- Lead: Most people start with one and end up doing all three. That's fine. Each one stands on its own.

### 01 Find the gap
I look at how the work really gets done: the hand-offs, the copy and paste, the things one person keeps in their head. Then I tell you plainly where the time and money are leaking.
- **You get:** a short, ranked list of the gaps worth fixing, what each one costs you now, and what fixing it looks like.
- **Suits:** owners and leaders who know something's off but can't put a finger on it.

### 02 Build the fix
I build the AI system, tool or automation that closes the gap, around the way your team already works. Fast, and in the open, so you see it working early.
- **You get:** a working system, and every account, key and line of it in your name.
- **Suits:** teams with a clear bottleneck and no time to wait months for it.

### 03 Teach your team
Hands-on training built around your real work, not slides. Everyone builds something useful in the first session.
- **You get:** a team that uses AI properly and can build the next fix themselves.
- **Suits:** businesses, schools and creators who've been told to "use AI" and nobody's shown them how.

### For schools
I train staff to use AI in their own work, and help schools keep students learning and thinking when AI's in the room. The aim is kids who love AI and think harder because of it, not less.

### For creators
You're the talent. With the right set-up, AI can be your researcher, editor, scheduler and analyst. I help you build that back room.

- Closing: Not sure which one you need? That's what the first call is for.
